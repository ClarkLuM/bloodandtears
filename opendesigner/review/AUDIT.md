# Audit: a more refined, modern look (16 Nov 2026)

Measured on the rendered test site (Roster, Records, Activity, a character page, Dungeons, Professions, Cards) at 1280 and 390 px wide, dark theme, and read in `public/index.html`. Colours and text stay as they are; this is about weight, size, corners, spacing and surfaces.

**Verdict:** the site looks busy rather than refined mainly because it uses two heavy weights side by side, too many slightly-different text sizes, and a dozen corner shapes. Fixing those three changes the most for the least work.

| # | Where | Before: what is wrong | After: the fix | Why: what practice says (source) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weights in use on every page: 400, 600 and 700 (measured; Roster 128 / 80 / 96 text runs). Code: `font-weight:700` ×53, `600` ×47, `500` ×1 | Bold and semi-bold sit next to each other, so headings, names, labels and numbers all shout a little and nothing leads | Two weights: 400 for reading text, 600 for headings, names, labels and numbers. 700 goes (the page titles keep their own small-caps face) | Two weights per view, clearly apart; neighbouring weights read as a mistake, bold everywhere reads as shouting (DC-L19-24; preflight 6) |
| 2 | Text sizes: 11 on the Roster (11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 22, 24, 32 px), 21 declared in the code (.4375 to 2.75rem) | Many near-twins (12 and 13, 14 and 15, 16 and 17, 20 and 22) make the hierarchy fuzzy | One scale: 11 (short labels only), 12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 32 px. Each size moves to its nearest step | About 8 sizes, none within 10% of another (preflight 7) |
| 3 | Corners: 16 radius values in the code (2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 96px, 1rem, 999px, 50%) | Cards, chips, buttons and tiles each round a little differently, which reads as hand-made | Four steps: 4px for tags and small details, 6px for buttons and fields, 10px for cards and panels, round for pills and faces | A short radius scale where each step has a named job; same kind of part, same corner (DC-L04-01; STD-visual-details-36) |
| 4 | Gaps off the 4px grid: .3125rem (5px) ×4, .1875rem (3px) ×3, .5625rem (9px), .4375rem (7px), 9px gaps on Records tiles (measured) | Small uneven spacing makes rows feel slightly crooked | Every gap on the 4px ladder: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px | All spacing from one scale; every value defensible (preflight 12; STD-visual-details-27) |
| 5 | `.card` and `main .card` (index.html): every panel has a 1px border **and** a long dark drop shadow (`0 10px 24px -14px`) | Panels feel heavy and boxed-in; the page is a stack of outlined boxes | Lighter surfaces: keep the hairline border, swap the drop shadow for a soft 1–2px one, and give panels a little more inner room (20px / 24px) | Modern work tools separate panels with a hairline and space, not heavy shadow (Linear and Vercel's dashboards: an example, not a rule) |
| 6 | Smallest text: 8–9px on the gear sheet slot names and card art, 10px on Professions numbers (measured) | Hard to read, especially on phones | 11px minimum for short labels, 12px for everything else (the card art keeps its own sizes: it's a printed card) | 12px on the web, 11px only for short labels (preflight 9) |

Not changed: the colours, the two typefaces (Figtree plus the small-caps page titles), the layout of each page. Checked and fine: one filled button per view (Roster "My character", character page "This is me"), contrast passes in both themes, focus rings are visible.
